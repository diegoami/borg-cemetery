These eight retained packages are four encounter states executed under two
pinned targets: source preparation `aae2c194` and accepted main `6d1bca10`.
They are **fresh-Borg counterfactuals of four original natural lives**, not eight
new population samples. Each package's canonical ID is mapped to its parent
natural-death ID, scenario and target in `lineage.json`. Repeated trials of the
same input/target have matching streams and remain replay evidence.

Source/native exporter records and every payload byte are preserved through
qualification. Its generic historical independence text does not override this
explicit saved-branch lineage. Generated registry indexes are lookup aids,
not independent natural population counts. Test reports and starts are under
`synthetic/fast-caster-encounters/`.
