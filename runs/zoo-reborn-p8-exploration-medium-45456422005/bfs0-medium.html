Two deaths with breadth-first off

Breadth-first off reached CL5 by 100,000 turns on 252/270 starts, versus 201/270 shipped. Two CL2 characters died with breadth-first off and survived in both other arms. The screens below reproduce both deaths exactly.

Both pairs first split over torches in town

At turn 421 for the Ranger (key 101), and turn 441 for the Warrior (key 96), breadth-first off left the General Store after buying three torches. Both other arms bought two more. The breadth-first flag also controls the early food and fuel multiplier, so this comparison changes shopping before it changes dungeon routes. Both characters still had light when they died. The early split changes the path to the encounters and does not establish which isolated change would prevent either death.

All six key streams matched the Zoo archive. The Warrior’s surviving pairs reached CL6 (first CL5 at turns 71,529 shipped and 53,854 with mixed off). The Ranger’s two surviving pairs played identical keys and reached CL4.

Dúnadan Warrior · giant white mouse · seed 480049625

At turn 12,857, the Warrior moved at 8/30 HP beside mice and an apprentice confused it. The Phase combat rule saw danger 2 without regional fear and declined escape until HP fell to 2. The landing check then rated all 100 samples scary. Its rejection did not list never-seen grids. No cure was available, and the Warrior died holding 16 Phase Door scrolls.

Both stair searches failed. The trace then selected boost_bravery_2 repeatedly, including the last move at 0 HP. Attempts to walk into the mouse while confused could turn into a wall hit or a spin. The terminal failure was a damaged, confused character boxed in without a route it could take or a landing the escape code would accept.

High-Elf Ranger · soldier · seed 1125613689

The Ranger met the first soldier while already routing toward the up stairs. The fight rule allowed Phase Door at 25/25 HP. At turn 9,834, 91 of 100 landings were never seen, and 92 were rated scary against a tolerance of 75. Similar refusals were followed by four melee attempts. The first accepted Phase Door came at 6 HP, then two more were used within 211 engine turns.

After the last scroll at turn 10,069, the Ranger briefly explored, then took the any-stair flow at 5/25 HP. The final trace estimated danger 0 when it moved with a soldier two steps away. Two hits killed it on turn 10,108. The pack still held two Cure Light Wounds and 25 arrows. The accurate callout is that the cure went unused: the final flow saw no danger and the earlier emergency actions used Phase Door. The traces do not log every rejected heal branch.
